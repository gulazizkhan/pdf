GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

A few details before you get started.

Are these tools free to use?

This starter version is free to try. Hosting, usage limits and future services may have separate costs.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

Implemented tools work in your browser. PDF libraries are loaded from third-party CDNs. Avoid processing sensitive documents on any service unless you have reviewed its security and privacy arrangements.

Why is PDF compression limited?

Rewriting a PDF does not necessarily reduce its size. Effective compression often requires recompressing embedded images and more specialized processing.

Does ToolNest support OCR and password-protected PDFs?

Not in this starter build. OCR, password encryption and unlocking require additional libraries or a carefully designed backend.
